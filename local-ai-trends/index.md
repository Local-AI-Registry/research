# LocalAITrends: what America searches before it books

  By the [Local AI Registry Research Team](https://localairegistry.com#authors)

  The short answer
  LocalAITrends is Local AI Registry’s running series of fun, checked patterns in what Americans search before they visit a local business. The first 34 trends show Botox search interest peaking the week before Thanksgiving, 28% to 50% above Thanksgiving week in each of the last five years, and lip filler and Pilates searches moving together at 0.94. When we tested 14 such “search twins” further, 13 fell apart. Every trend is labeled as a real pattern or just for fun.

  
- **34** trends so far
- **5** years of Google search data
- **49** states compared
- **13 of 14** search twins fell apart

## Key findings

- Local AI Registry found that U.S. Botox search interest in the week before Thanksgiving beat Thanksgiving week by 28% to 50% in every year from 2021 to 2025.
- The day before Thanksgiving, “nail salon near me” searches run 1.7 to 1.9 times a normal Wednesday, and on Thanksgiving Day “stretchy pants” searches jump 6 to 10 times a normal day, according to Local AI Registry.
- Thanksgiving Day was the top day for “liquor store near me” searches from mid-November to April two years running, at 2.4 times New Year’s Eve, Local AI Registry found.
- Across 49 states, green bean casserole search interest runs opposite to “botox near me” (rank correlation −0.46), while Tofurky runs with it (0.53), according to Local AI Registry.
- Spa gift card searches peak on Christmas Eve or Christmas Day, two years running, at about 4 times early-December levels, Local AI Registry found.
- Lip filler and Pilates searches moved together at 0.94 over five years, the closest of 14 viral “search twins” Local AI Registry published.
- When Local AI Registry also tested those 14 search twins on states and the age mix of searchers, 13 fell apart and only nose job and tarot held up.
- States that search Tesla the most also search facelifts the most (0.83), followed by Rolex (0.82) and “sushi near me” (0.80), which points to income and city life as the shared factor, according to Local AI Registry.
- States that search Real Housewives more also search “botox near me” more (rank correlation 0.56), the strongest TV pairing Local AI Registry found.

## What is LocalAITrends?
LocalAITrends is Local AI Registry’s running series of short data stories about how people look for local businesses, from Botox and nail salons to liquor stores and pizza.

Some trends are real patterns: they repeat every year and help a business plan its calendar. Others are coincidences that make you laugh, like lip filler and Pilates. We label every one so you always know which is which.

- Real pattern repeats year after year, or holds up when we test it further.
- Just for fun two lines that move together for no proven reason. Correlation, not causation.
- Held up a fun pair that surprised us and passed our extra tests.
The idea comes from data blogs like OkCupid’s OkTrends, which turned real user data into stories people wanted to share. Our version is about local businesses, and it is growing into what AI assistants say about them too.

## How we make each trend
Every number comes from a dated pull of U.S. Google search data, and every chart says how it was scaled.

- **Over time:** weekly or daily Google Trends search interest, December 2021 to November 2026. On daily charts marked “pulled together,” all lines share one scale, so you can compare their size.
- **Across states:** Google Trends search interest by state over the past 12 months, for 49 states (Wyoming and DC left out because tiny search counts push them to the top).
- **Gift searches:** Google Ads monthly U.S. search volumes for December 2025.
- **Extra tests:** for search twins, we also checked whether the two searches are big in the same states and searched by the same ages, the method from [Med Spa Search Signals](https://localairegistry.com/research/med-spa-search-signals#search-twins).
Data came through DataForSEO, pulled November 14 to 15, 2026. The data behind every chart is in a [CSV download](https://localairegistry.com/research/local-ai-trends/data/local-ai-trends-data.csv).

## What do Americans search for Thanksgiving week?
Botox the week before, the nail salon on Wednesday, stretchy pants and the liquor store on Thursday. Three of these four repeat every year.

![Bar chart of U.S. Botox search interest, week before Thanksgiving vs Thanksgiving week: 2021 75 vs 50, 2022 70 vs 51, 2023 87 vs 60, 2024 83 vs 65, 2025 92 vs 70](https://localairegistry.com/research/local-ai-trends/images/botox-search-interest-week-before-thanksgiving.png)

*Botox searches peak the week before Thanksgiving, five years running.* In every year from 2021 to 2025, Botox search interest in the week before Thanksgiving beat Thanksgiving week by 28% to 50%. In 2026 that week is November 15 to 21.
People book Botox ahead of family photos, since results take several days to show. In 2026 the peak week is November 15 to 21, right now.

![Line chart of daily U.S. search interest, Nov 11 to Dec 8, 2024: nail salon near me peaks on the day before Thanksgiving, stretchy pants jumps on Thanksgiving Day, tummy tuck stays flat](https://localairegistry.com/research/local-ai-trends/images/nail-salon-searches-day-before-thanksgiving-stretchy-pants.png)

*Nail salon searches spike the day before Thanksgiving; stretchy pants spike on the day.* The day before Thanksgiving, “nail salon near me” searches run 1.7 to 1.9 times a normal Wednesday (2024 and 2025). On Thanksgiving Day, “stretchy pants” searches jump 6 to 10 times a normal day. Tummy tuck searches do not move.

![Line chart of daily U.S. search interest, Nov 15, 2025 to Apr 30, 2026: liquor store near me peaks at 100 on Thanksgiving; dispensary near me reaches 37 on Thanksgiving and Christmas and 26 on 4/20](https://localairegistry.com/research/local-ai-trends/images/liquor-store-dispensary-searches-thanksgiving-vs-4-20.png)

*Thanksgiving is the top day for liquor store searches and rivals 4/20 for dispensaries.* Thanksgiving Day was the top day for “liquor store near me” from mid-November to April, two years running, at 2.4 times New Year’s Eve. Dispensary searches on Thanksgiving and Christmas (37 and 37) beat 4/20 (26) this past season. One unexplained spike to 52 on April 22 is the season’s highest dispensary day, so treat single days with care.
The likely reason is “who’s open today?” Many stores close or cut hours on the holiday, so people check before they drive. Industry coverage still calls 4/20 the biggest sales day for cannabis, so read this as searches, not sales.

![Scatter plot of 49 states: green bean casserole search interest against botox near me search interest, sloping down; Montana and Nebraska high on casserole and low on Botox, New Jersey and New York the reverse](https://localairegistry.com/research/local-ai-trends/images/green-bean-casserole-states-vs-botox-searches-by-state.png)

*Green bean casserole states search Botox less (−0.46); Tofurky states search it more (0.53).* Across 49 states, green bean casserole search interest runs opposite to “botox near me” (rank correlation −0.46) and to “plastic surgeon near me” (−0.54). Tofurky runs with Botox (0.53). City versus country is the likely real link.

## What do people search when they shop for beauty and spa gifts?
Gadgets on Black Friday, perfume in December, spa gift cards on Christmas Eve, and red light therapy after the unwrapping.

![Line chart of weekly U.S. search interest, Oct 2025 to Jan 2026: silk pillowcase peaks Black Friday week, perfume sampler peaks mid-December, red light therapy peaks the week after Christmas](https://localairegistry.com/research/local-ai-trends/images/beauty-gift-search-calendar-black-friday-perfume-red-light.png)

*Beauty gift searches come in three waves: gadgets, perfume, then red light therapy.* Two holiday seasons in a row, silk pillowcases peak on Black Friday week, perfume samplers build to mid-December, and red light therapy peaks the week after Christmas. Each line is scaled to its own peak in this window.

![Line chart of daily U.S. search interest, Black Friday to Dec 31, 2024: spa gift card climbs to 98 on Christmas Eve and 100 on Christmas Day; perfume gift set runs high all month](https://localairegistry.com/research/local-ai-trends/images/spa-gift-card-searches-peak-christmas-eve.png)

*Spa gift card searches peak on Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.* Spa gift card searches peak on Christmas Eve or Christmas Day, two years running, at about 4 times early-December levels. Perfume gift sets run high all month from Black Friday.

- **Girlfriends get jewelry, wives get lingerie, moms get the spa day.** For every 100 “gifts for mom” searches, about 4.9 were for a spa day, the most of anyone. Spa days for husbands and boyfriends got 30 and 10 searches all month. Google Ads volumes, December 2025; rounded estimates. ![Bar chart of December 2025 gift searches per 100 gift searches: jewelry 7.3 for girlfriend, spa day 4.9 for mom, lingerie 1.8 for wife](https://localairegistry.com/research/local-ai-trends/images/holiday-gift-searches-jewelry-lingerie-spa-day.jpg)
- **Gifts for her are a Christmas Eve scramble.** On Christmas Eve, “gift for wife” searches ran 2.4 to 2.8 times “gift for husband” (2024 and 2025). The data shows who the gift is for, not who is searching. ![Card: on Christmas Eve, gift for wife searches ran 2.4 to 2.8 times gift for husband searches](https://localairegistry.com/research/local-ai-trends/images/gift-for-wife-vs-husband-searches-christmas-eve.jpg)

## Which days of the year send people to local shops?
Friday the 13th is tattoo day, six for six since September 2024.

![Line chart of daily U.S. search interest, Oct 16 to Nov 13, 2026: tattoo near me spikes on Friday, November 13; piercing near me rises less; botox near me stays flat](https://localairegistry.com/research/local-ai-trends/images/tattoo-near-me-searches-friday-the-13th.png)

*Tattoo searches run 2.2 to 3.2 times a normal Friday on Friday the 13th.* On each of the last six Friday the 13ths, “tattoo near me” searches ran 2.2 to 3.2 times a normal Friday. On November 13, 2026 it came in at 3.0 times. The next one is August 13, 2027.
Many tattoo shops run Friday the 13th flash specials, and the searches show it. Botox and hair salon searches stayed flat on the same days.

## Did the search twins hold up?
No. When we also checked states and the age mix of searchers, 13 of the 14 fell apart. Nose job and tarot held up.

![Waffle of 14 squares: 1 green square for nose job and tarot, which passed timing, state and age tests; 13 grey squares for pairs that failed](https://localairegistry.com/research/local-ai-trends/images/viral-search-twins-that-held-up-waffle.png)

*Only 1 of 14 viral search twins held up when we tested it further.* All 14 search twins were picked because their five-year charts matched. When we also checked states and the age mix of searchers, 13 fell apart. Nose job and tarot held up.
A matching chart is only the first test. Two searches that share an audience should also be big in the same states and searched by people of the same age. In a wider test of 680 beauty and everyday pairs, only two passed everything: nose job with tarot, and Botox with wedding dress. The full method is in [Med Spa Search Signals](https://localairegistry.com/research/med-spa-search-signals#search-twins).

## What do these trends mean for a local business?
The real patterns are a calendar. People search before they book, so be easy to find before the busy day.

TakeawayPlan for the search, not the holiday itself.

### What to do
Check your hours, booking link and holiday specials on Google and your website a week before the busy day. Botox searches peak the week before Thanksgiving, nail salon searches the day before, and liquor store searches on the day itself.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is LocalAITrends?
LocalAITrends is Local AI Registry’s series of short, fun data stories about what people search before they visit a local business. Each trend comes from real search data and is labeled either a real pattern, which repeats year after year, or just for fun, which is a coincidence worth a laugh.

### Where does the LocalAITrends data come from?
From Google Trends and Google Ads search volumes for the United States, pulled through DataForSEO in November 2026. The numbers measure search interest, not sales, bookings or visits.

### What does a match score mean?
A match score shows how closely two lines move together, from 1.00 (exactly together) through 0 (no link) to −1.00 (exact opposites). Over time, it compares five years of monthly search interest. Across states, it compares how 49 states rank on each search.

### Does lip filler cause Pilates?
No. Two searches can rise together because something else drives both, such as summer, wedding season, income or city life. That is why most LocalAITrends cards say “Correlation, not causation.”

### Why do Botox searches peak before Thanksgiving?
People want to look rested before family gatherings and holiday photos, and Botox takes several days to show results. In every year from 2021 to 2025, the week before Thanksgiving beat Thanksgiving week by 28% to 50%.

### Which search twins held up?
Only nose job and tarot. Of the 14 viral pairs, 13 fell apart once we also checked states and the age mix of searchers. In a wider test of 680 pairs, only nose job with tarot and Botox with wedding dress passed all three tests, as reported in Med Spa Search Signals.

### What does this have to do with AI?
AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews now answer many of these same local questions. The next LocalAITrends entries will come from AI answers themselves: what AI recommends, where and how often. Local AI Registry’s earlier study, The Local Answer Gap, already looked at 7,583 Google AI answers about local businesses.

## Glossary

- **Search interest**: Google Trends’ 0 to 100 score for how often a term is searched, where 100 is that term’s busiest week, day or state in the window.
- **Match score**: A correlation between two lines, from 1.00 (move exactly together) to −1.00 (move in opposite directions).
- **Rank correlation**: A match score that compares how 49 states rank on two searches, so one outlier state cannot drive the result.
- **Search twin**: Two unrelated searches whose five-year charts look almost the same.
- **Correlation, not causation**: Two things moving together does not mean one causes the other.
- **Hidden third factor**: Something else, such as summer or income, that pushes two searches up at the same time and makes them look linked.
- **Real pattern**: A LocalAITrends label for a pattern that repeats across years or holds up under further tests.
- **AEO (answer engine optimization)**: Work that helps a business get found, described correctly and recommended by AI assistants.

## Limits of this research

- Google Trends shows relative interest, not counts. Each line is scaled to its own peak unless a chart says the lines were pulled together.
- Search is not sales. A spike in “liquor store near me” likely means people checking holiday hours, not record sales.
- State comparisons describe states, not people. A Tesla state that searches facelifts does not mean Tesla owners get facelifts.
- We tested many pairs and show the fun ones. Some matches are luck, which is the point of the “just for fun” label.
- The newest days in Google’s data, such as November 13, 2026, can still shift a little. Single-day spikes can be data blips; the dispensary series has one on April 22, 2026.
- All data is for the United States.
