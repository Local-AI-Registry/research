Med Spa Search Signals: what Google searches reveal about aesthetic clients and sales

By the Local AI Registry Research Team

The short answer

Local AI Registry’s analysis of five years of Google searches shows that a big brand with the same shopper can read a small brand’s sales early. Growth in Sephora searches tracked growth in Jeuveau maker Evolus’s revenue at 0.71 over 15 quarters, while Jeuveau’s own searches scored −0.08. Using Sephora, we estimate Evolus’s Q4 2026 revenue at about $107 million, above the $95 million to $102 million implied by guidance. The same data shows med spa clients live on the Pilates map, men search for surgery but not upkeep, and January to March is research season.

  • 94 searches tested as stand-ins
  • 15 quarters of company sales
  • 49 states compared
  • 680 search pairs tested
  • 41 charts, maps, explainers and 3D visuals

Key findings

  1. Local AI Registry found that growth in U.S. Google searches for Sephora tracked growth in Evolus revenue (the maker of Jeuveau) at 0.71 across 15 quarters, while searches for Jeuveau itself scored −0.08.
  2. Sephora averages about 6.1 million U.S. Google searches a month and Jeuveau about 18,100, so Sephora’s search trend is 338 times larger to read, Local AI Registry found.
  3. Using last quarter’s growth plus Sephora searches, Local AI Registry’s estimate landed within 7% of Evolus revenue in 11 of 14 quarters and puts Q4 2026 revenue at about $107M, above the $95M to $102M implied by company guidance.
  4. Equinox searches moved opposite to Jeuveau sales over five years (−0.78) but showed no link quarter to quarter (0.02), so a long-trend match alone can mislead, according to Local AI Registry.
  5. In 2025, searches for “botox” rose 10% to 16% year over year while Botox Cosmetic sales fell 5% to 12%, which shows “botox” works as a category word, Local AI Registry found.
  6. Club Pilates brand searches fell 17% year over year in Q3 2026 while Pilates searches were still up 17% in Q2 2026, and Xponential reported same-store sales down 7.5%, according to Local AI Registry.
  7. Across 49 states, Local AI Registry found that searches for med spa treatments follow the same state ranking as Pilates (0.81), Tesla and Rolex (0.75), and run opposite to crockpots and fishing licenses (−0.66).
  8. Men make up about 46% of searches for liposuction, facelifts and nose jobs but 22% to 33% of searches for Botox, nails, lashes and spray tans, Local AI Registry found.
  9. Local AI Registry found that Botox searches hold near their yearly average in January to March (102) while Botox sales to clinics run 8% below it (92).
  10. Of 680 pairs of beauty and everyday searches, only 2 matched on timing, states and age mix, and only 1 of 14 viral “search twin” pairs held up, according to Local AI Registry.

What is a search signal, and why borrow one from a bigger brand?

A search signal is a trend in how often people search for something on Google. When searches for a product rise, sales often follow. The problem is that many brands are searched too rarely to read. Jeuveau, the wrinkle-relaxing injection made by Evolus, averages about 18,100 U.S. searches a month. Sephora averages about 6.1 million.

So we borrowed the signal. We looked for a much bigger search that shares Jeuveau’s customer and moves with Evolus’s sales. Then we used it to estimate a quarter Evolus has not reported yet. Along the way, we used the same data to describe who the med spa client is, where they live, when they research and what else they buy.

Terms used in this study

Search signal
A trend in how often people search for something on Google, used as an early read on what they will buy.
Proxy search
A search for a bigger brand or topic with the same customers, used when the brand you care about gets too few searches to read.
Nowcast
An estimate of a period that has already started but has not been reported yet, such as a quarter's sales before the earnings release.
Year-over-year growth
The change against the same period a year earlier, which removes normal seasonal ups and downs.
Correlation
A number from −1 to 1 that shows how closely two series move together. It shows a match, not a cause.
Category word
A word people use for a whole type of product, the way “botox” is used for any wrinkle-relaxing injection.
Search twin
Two unrelated searches whose charts move together. We test them on timing, states and age mix to see if the same people are behind them.
Answer engine optimization (AEO)
Making a business’s facts clear, consistent and machine-readable so AI systems such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews include it accurately.

Has anyone used other brands' searches to estimate aesthetics sales?

Using Google searches to read the economy early is an established idea. Hal Varian and Hyunyoung Choi showed in 2012 that Google Trends data improves near-term estimates of retail sales, car sales and travel. Sharad Goel and colleagues showed in 2010 that searches anticipate box office, game and music sales.

Those studies use searches for the product itself. We could not find published work that estimates aesthetic injectable sales from another brand’s searches, or that tests the med spa client against everyday searches on timing, states and age at once. That is the gap this study fills.

How we did this research

  • Search data: weekly U.S. Google Trends interest for 101 searches from November 2021 to November 2026, each pulled on its own scale, via DataForSEO. 94 had five full years of usable data and were tested as stand-ins.
  • Search volume: average monthly U.S. Google searches from Google Ads, November 2025 to October 2026, via DataForSEO.
  • Sales: Evolus total net revenue for each quarter from Q1 2022 to Q3 2026 and its 2026 guidance of $330 million to $337 million; AbbVie global Botox Cosmetic net revenue from Q1 2023 to Q3 2026 (Q4 2023 and Q4 2024 are the full year minus the first three quarters); Xponential Fitness results for Q3 2025 to Q3 2026.
  • Stand-in test: for each search, we compared growth against the same quarter a year before with Evolus’s revenue growth (15 quarters), then compared the quarter-to-quarter changes in those growth rates. A useful stand-in has to pass both.
  • Estimate: Evolus revenue growth predicted from last quarter’s growth and Sephora search growth, tested by hiding one quarter at a time. The Q4 2026 estimate uses Sephora searches from October 4 to November 14, 2026, against the same weeks of 2025.
  • States: Google Trends state interest over the past five years for 49 states (Wyoming and Washington, D.C. left out because of small samples). The med spa score averages eight treatment searches after putting each on the same scale. Matches are rank correlations.
  • People: DataForSEO’s search panel age and gender mix for 59 searches over the past 12 months, and BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey 2024 personal care spending by age.
  • Search twins: 17 beauty searches against 40 everyday searches (680 pairs), tested on timing (monthly match of 0.50 or more, with and without long trends), states (rank match of 0.35 or more) and age mix (85% overlap or more).
  • All aggregated tables are in the CSV download.

Can Sephora searches predict Jeuveau sales?

Yes, better than Jeuveau’s own searches. Sephora search growth matched Evolus revenue growth at 0.71 over 15 quarters; Jeuveau searches scored −0.08.

We tested 94 searches, from Sephora and Ulta to Equinox, Pilates, Wingstop and Taylor Swift. Three passed both tests: Sephora, espresso martini and Taylor Swift. Testing this many searches means some will match by luck, so we only use a stand-in with a real link to the same shopper. Of the three, only Sephora has one.

Equinox is the trap. Over five years its searches move opposite to Jeuveau sales (−0.78), which looks like a gym-versus-injectables budget fight. Quarter to quarter there is no link (0.02). The match came from two slow trends pointing different ways. “Equinox” is also a car and a season, which muddies its searches further.

AEO takeaway

A big brand with the same customer can be a better gauge than your own brand name.

What to do

If your brand or category is too small to read in search, find a large search that shares your customer, and check it against your own sales before you rely on it.

What do searches say about Jeuveau's Q4 2026 sales?

They point above guidance. Our estimate for Evolus’s Q4 2026 revenue is about $107 million; the company’s guidance implies $95 million to $102 million.

Sephora searches from October 4 to November 14, 2026, are up 7.5% on the same weeks of 2025. With last quarter’s growth of 13.1%, that points to Q4 revenue up about 18%, roughly $101 million to $113 million. Evolus guided to $330 million to $337 million for 2026 and has reported $235.2 million through Q3.

Search moved in the same quarter as sales in our tests, not a quarter ahead. So this is an early read, available about seven weeks before Evolus last shared preliminary Q4 numbers (January 9, 2026). It is research, not investment advice.

AEO takeaway

Search gives an early read on a quarter before the books close.

What to do

Compare searches for your business and your category with the same weeks last year. A clear gap is an early warning or an early win.

Do searches for a brand track that brand's sales?

For brand names, yes. For category words, no. “botox” rose in 2025 while Botox Cosmetic sales fell; Jeuveau searches matched Evolus’s sales direction in 10 of 11 quarters.

Club Pilates shows the same rule from the other side. Brand searches grew 15% in Q1 2025, stalled by mid-2025 and fell 17% by Q3 2026. Xponential’s North America same-store sales turned negative in Q3 2025 (down 1%) and kept falling, to down 7.5% in Q3 2026 (Club Pilates down 5.6%). Plain “pilates” searches were still up 17% in Q2 2026. The category grew; the brand did not.

AEO takeaway

Your name is the number to watch, next to your category.

What to do

Track searches for your business name against searches for what you sell. If your name falls while the category holds, a competitor is taking share.

Where do med spa clients live?

In the same states that search for Pilates. Our combined med spa score matches Pilates search by state at 0.81.

Florida, Arizona, Colorado, Utah and California lead the med spa score; Vermont, South Dakota, Iowa and West Virginia trail. The everyday searches that share the map (Pilates, Tesla, Rolex, Uber Eats, sushi) and the ones that oppose it (crockpots, fishing licenses, air fryers) suggest much of this is the map of city life and income. Treat it as a wallet signal, not a cause.

The ring maps put two searches on one map. Med spa states are Pilates states (0.81) and Sephora states (0.74), but not Equinox states (−0.54). So Sephora passes on place as well as on time, and Equinox fails on both.

AEO takeaway

Med spa demand is concentrated, and it sits next to fitness and luxury spending.

What to do

Put more budget in the states and cities where your treatment and your partners are searched most.

Who searches for aesthetic treatments?

Men search for surgery about as often as for anything else (46%) but rarely for upkeep (22% to 33%). Lip filler searchers are about 33; Botox searchers about 41.

The youngest treatment searchers sit in the households that spend least on personal care: $647 a year under 25 and $863 at 25 to 34, against about $1,200 at 35 to 54. In a 2026 Zenoti survey, 26% of Gen Z had delayed medical or dental care to pay for beauty, against 11% of boomers. For younger treatments, payment plans and memberships matter most.

AEO takeaway

Different treatments draw different people.

What to do

Write for the real searcher: surgery pages for men as well as women, and clear prices and payment options for the youngest treatments.

When do clients research, and when do they buy?

Clients research all year, including January to March. Botox searches hold at 102 in Q1 while Botox sales to clinics drop to 92.

These are AbbVie’s sales to practices, not what patients pay, so clinics stocking up in Q4 explains part of the Q1 dip. Even so, searches do not take a break after the holidays.

AEO takeaway

Going quiet in January means going quiet while people decide.

What to do

Keep marketing in January to March, when searches hold steady and competitors pull back.

What else do aesthetic clients spend on?

Dining out, clothing, nails and spas, in that order of what they would cut first. Treatments come last: 24% would cut them.

Card-level spending data on med spa clients is not public. These public sources point the same way: wedding, dating and fitness businesses share the most with Botox searchers. Wine matches best on states, but it falls under dining out, the first thing clients cut. Weight-loss drug searches share the age mix but not the states. In a 2026 Morning Consult survey for LaserAway, 53% of U.S. adults said they would give up everyday spending to afford non-surgical treatments.

AEO takeaway

The best partners share your client and survive budget cuts.

What to do

Start with bridal shops and Pilates or fitness studios. Test drinks or dining with a one-off event before a standing deal.

Which search twins share a real audience?

Very few. Of 680 beauty and everyday search pairs, 2 matched on timing, states and age mix: nose job with tarot, and Botox with wedding dress. Of our 14 viral pairs, only nose job and tarot held up.

Frequently asked questions

Can Google searches predict a company's sales?

Sometimes, and best when the search is a big brand with the same customers. In Local AI Registry’s study, Sephora search growth matched Evolus revenue growth at 0.71 over 15 quarters, and an estimate built on it landed within 7% of actual sales in 11 of 14 quarters. Search gives an early read on direction more reliably than on exact size.

Why use Sephora searches to estimate Jeuveau sales?

Because almost nobody searches for Jeuveau: about 18,100 U.S. searches a month against 6.1 million for Sephora. Sephora shares the same beauty shopper and its search trend is steady enough to read. Of 94 searches tested, Sephora was the only beauty brand that matched Evolus revenue on both the long trend and the quarter-to-quarter changes.

Do Equinox or gym searches predict Botox or Jeuveau spending?

No. Equinox searches moved opposite to Jeuveau sales over five years (−0.78), but quarter to quarter they showed no link (0.02). Planet Fitness and “botox near me” failed the same test. A long-trend match alone can come from two unrelated drifts.

What is Local AI Registry's Q4 2026 estimate for Evolus?

About $107 million, with a likely range of $101 million to $113 million. Evolus’s 2026 guidance of $330 million to $337 million implies $95 million to $102 million for Q4. This is research, not investment advice; Evolus’s own results will show whether it was right.

Do more Botox searches mean more Botox sales?

Not for the Botox brand. In 2025, searches for “botox” rose 10% to 16% while Botox Cosmetic sales fell 5% to 12%. People use “botox” for any wrinkle-relaxing injection, so the word tracks the whole category. Searches for the brand name Jeuveau matched its maker’s sales direction in 10 of 11 quarters.

Which states search most for med spa treatments?

Florida, Arizona, Colorado, Utah and California lead Local AI Registry’s combined score of eight treatment searches. Vermont, South Dakota, Iowa and West Virginia trail. The same states lead searches for Pilates (0.81 match).

Do men search for cosmetic procedures?

Yes, especially surgery. Men make up about 46% of searches for liposuction, facelifts and nose jobs, close to their 44% median share of everyday searches. For Botox, nails, lashes and spray tans, their share is 22% to 33%.

When do people research Botox?

All year, including January to March. Botox searches stay near their yearly average in Q1 (102 against 100), while AbbVie’s Botox sales to clinics run 8% below average (92) and rise in Q2 (105).

What do aesthetic clients cut first when money is tight?

Dining out. In NewBeauty’s 2025 survey as reported by AmSpa, 54% of aesthetic clients would cut dining out, 43% clothing and 42% nail care, while only 24% would cut aesthetic treatments.

Which businesses should med spas partner with?

Bridal and dating-related businesses and Pilates or fitness studios. Wedding and dating searches pass the most of our timing, state and age tests with “botox near me”, and fitness shares the same states and age mix and is one of the last things clients cut.

What is a search twin?

Two unrelated searches whose charts move together, like nose job and tarot. Of 680 pairs Local AI Registry tested, only 2 also matched on states and age mix: nose job with tarot, and Botox with wedding dress.

Limits of this research

  • Google Trends shows relative interest, not counts, and each search is scaled to its own peak. Weekly values for small searches are noisy.
  • We tested 94 searches against one company’s revenue over 15 quarters. With that many tests and that few quarters, some matches are luck. Taylor Swift and espresso martini matched almost as well as Sephora.
  • Evolus revenue includes its newer filler line, and AbbVie reports global Botox Cosmetic sales while we measured U.S. search.
  • The Q4 2026 estimate uses about six weeks of searches and does not include the holiday weeks. It is research, not investment advice.
  • Age and gender come from a search panel, not from Google, and some age bands for small searches are empty. Read them as relative.
  • State matches mostly reflect where people live and how much they earn. They show shared places, not that one search causes another.
  • Spending and survey figures come from the published sources named here; we did not have card-level transaction data.
