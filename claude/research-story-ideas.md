# Local AI Registry Research: Story Ideas and Test Pulls

Prepared by the Local AI Registry research team. Test pulls run November 13, 2026 (Pacific), which is November 14, 2026 00:03 to 00:06 UTC in the DataForSEO logs.

Goals key:
- **G1** Journalist coverage and links (a dated, citable finding a reporter can use today)
- **G2** Being the answer in AI (original data that ChatGPT and others cite for "how do I get my business into AI")

Every story ends with the button: **[How often does AI name you?](https://audit.localairegistry.com/home/look-up/)**

Ground rules followed: no invented stats; every number below traces to a pull in the Pull Log (bottom) with its DataForSEO task ID and date. Business names appear only as AI showed them. We do not rank businesses. Client check: none of the businesses named in these pulls is a Local AI Registry client.

## Step 1: 25 story ideas

Cost estimates use DataForSEO list rates and are rough.

**Human behavior**

1. **"People ask AI whether and how much. They ask Google where."** Hypothesis: decision questions (cost, safety, "is it worth it", "how to choose") get AI volume close to Google's, while "near me" searches barely exist in AI. Pull: AI keyword volume vs Google Ads volume, same 20 keywords, same month. Cost: about $0.10. Beat: tech, business. Goal: G1, G2.
2. **"The last question before the BBL."** Hypothesis: safety questions ("is BBL safe") outnumber price questions in AI. Pull: AI volume for 30 BBL question variants. Cost: about $0.05. Beat: health. Goal: G1.
3. **"Nobody asks AI for a plumber."** Hypothesis: emergency home services stay on Google and Maps while elective care moves to AI. Pull: AI vs Google volume for 10 home service and 10 elective care terms. Cost: about $0.10. Beat: local news, business. Goal: G1, G2.
4. **"Patients ask AI how to choose a dentist more than they ask Google."** Hypothesis: "how to choose" queries over-index in AI. Pull: AI vs Google volume across 6 verticals' "how to choose a ___". Cost: about $0.10. Beat: health, consumer. Goal: G1, G2.
5. **"What patients ask AI about Botox that they never type into Google."** Hypothesis: long conversational questions show up only in AI data. Pull: AI keyword ideas vs Google keyword ideas for "botox". Cost: about $0.30. Beat: health, beauty. Goal: G1.

**City rivalries**

6. **"America's Botox capital, per person."** Hypothesis: a non-coastal metro beats LA and Miami per capita. Pull: Google Ads "botox" volume in 25 metros plus Census population. Cost: about $2. Beat: local news. Goal: G1.
7. **"Which city asks about lip filler most."** Same method, lip filler. Cost: about $2. Beat: local news, beauty. Goal: G1.
8. **"The LASIK belt."** Hypothesis: LASIK interest per person is highest in sunny outdoor metros. Pull: city volumes plus Census. Cost: about $2. Beat: local news, health. Goal: G1.
9. **"Does AI know your town exists?"** Hypothesis: AI names far fewer local businesses for small cities than for big ones. Pull: ChatGPT "best med spa in [city]" for 10 big and 10 small cities. Cost: about $0.50. Beat: local news, tech. Goal: G1, G2.

**Seasonality and timing**

10. **"The FSA deadline LASIK rush."** Hypothesis: "LASIK FSA" searches spike around open enrollment and the March grace-period deadline. Pull: Google Ads monthly volume, FSA plus LASIK terms. Cost: about $0.08. Beat: health, personal finance. Goal: G1.
11. **"Botox before the wedding."** Hypothesis: wedding prep questions spike in spring. Pull: monthly Google and AI volume for wedding prep treatment terms. Cost: about $0.10. Beat: beauty, weddings. Goal: G1.
12. **"New Year's resolution med spa bump."** Hypothesis: January spike in body contouring questions. Pull: 24 months historical keyword data. Cost: about $0.20. Beat: health, business. Goal: G1.
13. **"Summer is LASIK season."** Hypothesis: LASIK cost questions peak before summer. Pull: monthly volumes, 24 months. Cost: about $0.10. Beat: health. Goal: G1.
14. **"The dispensary holiday curve."** Hypothesis: 4/20 and Thanksgiving Eve show spikes in AI and Google. Pull: monthly and Google Trends daily. Cost: about $0.20. Beat: business, cannabis. Goal: G1.

**AI behavior**

15. **"Ask ChatGPT the same question 3 times, get 3 different surgeons."** Hypothesis: AI recommendations are unstable run to run. Pull: ChatGPT scraper, same prompt, 3 runs, 2 verticals. Cost: about $0.30. Beat: tech, health. Goal: G1, G2.
16. **"ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity can't agree on a single surgeon."** Hypothesis: the three engines' lists barely overlap. Pull: same prompt to all three. Cost: about $0.10. Beat: tech. Goal: G1, G2.
17. **"AI's favorite med spa has 72 reviews."** Hypothesis: AI's top pick is not the most reviewed business. Pull: ChatGPT map pins with review counts. Cost: about $0.10. Beat: business, tech. Goal: G1, G2.
18. **"How many businesses does AI name before it stops?"** Hypothesis: AI answers almost always stop at 5, so a city of hundreds of providers gets 5 slots. Pull: count names across 50 ChatGPT answers. Cost: about $1.50. Beat: tech, business. Goal: G2.
19. **"AI answers in Spanish name different doctors."** Hypothesis: the same question in Spanish returns a different list. Pull: ChatGPT English vs Spanish, Miami and LA. Cost: about $0.30. Beat: tech, Latino community press. Goal: G1.

**AI trust**

20. **"Where AI gets its picks: list sites, not your website."** Hypothesis: AI cites third-party "best of" pages more than businesses' own sites. Pull: source domains from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini answers. Cost: reuses pulls from 15 and 16. Beat: tech, business. Goal: G2, G1.
21. **"A surgeon's own 'best surgeons' blog post is AI's evidence."** Hypothesis: AI cites self-published rankings. Pull: Gemini and Perplexity annotations. Cost: reuses pulls. Beat: tech, health. Goal: G1.
22. **"Yelp vs RealSelf vs your website: who AI believes."** Hypothesis: one platform dominates citations per vertical. Pull: 40 prompts, tally domains. Cost: about $1. Beat: business, tech. Goal: G2.

**AI versus Google**

23. **"Google's top result vs ChatGPT's top pick."** Hypothesis: they rarely match. Pull: Google organic and local pack vs ChatGPT for 20 city prompts. Cost: about $1. Beat: tech. Goal: G1, G2.
24. **"Comparison questions go to AI."** Hypothesis: "Botox vs Dysport" style questions over-index in AI. Pull: AI vs Google volume for 15 comparison terms. Cost: about $0.05. Beat: health, beauty. Goal: G1.
25. **"The $33 click AI gives away free."** Hypothesis: the most expensive Google ad keywords in healthcare are questions people now ask AI. Pull: Google CPC plus AI volume. Cost: about $0.10. Beat: business, marketing. Goal: G1, G2.

## Step 4: Story pitches for survivors

### Pitch 1: "Ask ChatGPT the same question three times, get three different plastic surgeons" (G1, G2)

On November 13, 2026, Local AI Registry asked ChatGPT "Who is the best plastic surgeon in Los Angeles?" three separate times. It named 12 different surgeons across 15 slots, no surgeon appeared all three times, and the first name changed every run. The same test for Miami med spas produced 10 different businesses across 15 slots, and ChatGPT's "if I had to pick one" choice flipped between two clinics, one with 72 reviews and one with 989. For patients, the takeaway is that one AI answer is a sample, not a verdict. For businesses, being named once means little; how often you are named is the real measure. Beat: tech, health. Disclosure: none of the businesses named is a Local AI Registry client.

### Pitch 2: "People ask AI whether and how much. They ask Google where." (G1, G2)

Estimated AI search data for October 2026 shows Americans bring their deciding questions to AI and their finding questions to Google. "How to choose a dentist" had an estimated 874 AI searches against 590 on Google. "How much is Botox" ran at 67% of Google's volume and "is LASIK worth it" at 72%. But "Botox near me" had 1 estimated AI search against 246,000 on Google, and "dentist near me" had 121 against 2.24 million. The decision about which provider to trust is moving into AI conversations before a patient ever opens a map. AI figures are DataForSEO modeled estimates of LLM usage, not company data. Beat: tech, business, health.
